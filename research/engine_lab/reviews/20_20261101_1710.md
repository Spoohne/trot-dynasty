## 2. Near-zero form and start luck

**Not inherently bad for outcomes. Potentially bad for the promised explanation.**

Because cruise/kick weights sum to one, form contributes exactly `+f` to performance. There, Gaussian form and Gaussian residual are interchangeable; they differ principally because form also changes starts and movement. The fit is choosing luck that mostly does **not** alter those earlier events.

And rerun luck is not all ε: first-over selection, the no-move draw and breaks remain random.

If “how it’s going today” must affect the whole race coherently, choose a modest form SD as a **design constraint**, then refit. Do not claim these aggregates estimated it. If that coherence is unnecessary, simplify the noise model rather than retaining a nearly unused narrative variable.

Near-zero start randomness also warrants a direct test: **how repetitive are early positions across reruns?** Winner repeat will not reveal that.
