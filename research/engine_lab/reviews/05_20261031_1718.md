## Astra

## 1. Listing fee and launch baseline

**Measured:** the $250k fee **under the current listing heuristic** restores concentration. That does not establish that every meaningful fee must do so: `house_price × 10 ≥ fee` directly imposes a quality cutoff.

**Recommendation:** target **broad effective access**, not “every stallion lists.” A meaningful choice will exclude some marginal listings.

Test two simple designs:
- **Small flat upfront fee**, with listing decisions based on estimated demand, net revenue and owner reserves—not house price.
- **Small upfront fee plus a fraction of realized stud revenue**, if Patrick approves that interpretation of paid listing. This places most cost on successful listings without requiring speculative owners to fund it first.

Avoid scaling solely to an owner-set stud fee: that invites underquoting unless transaction prices enforce it. A covers-sold charge alone is a transaction fee, not a costly listing decision.

Measure **distinct sires actually used**, covers/revenue concentration, access to useful nick partners, owner net income and failed bookings. Listing 600 unused stallions is not diversity. Compare demand-aware AI with the existing heuristic to separate fee effects from listing-policy effects.

**H / baseline recommendation:** start launch tests at **AI quality weight 0.3**, bracketed by 0.0 and 0.6. It currently offers an intermediate drift/diversity result while retaining positive quality selection. This is not yet evidence that it satisfies “best-to-best works”: test parent-quality and nick effects separately on matched mares. Use earned game funds only; no purchasable racing advantage.

## 2. Closers: isolate the deficit before widening search

**H8 remains plausible, not proven by 48 nearby configurations.** Re-baseline field-level conclusions after the start fix; isolated controls may survive, but should be rerun.

Two important **code findings**:

- Every horse requests sprint speed at **its own 75% distance**, regardless of `move_at`. Thus moving at 0.88 does not mean waiting until 0.88 to accelerate; it mainly delays commitment/lane behavior.
- `kick_late` multiplies **late-speed ability**, not closer style. With identical traits, closers get no intrinsic extra kick.

Run a paired **move point × traffic-access** assay: identical horses, posts rotated, fixed opponents, breaks disabled.

| Mechanism | Decisive intervention |
|---|---|
| Timing | Sweep move points 0.55/0.65/0.75/0.85; separately toggle the universal 0.75 sprint rule. Log requested and achieved acceleration onset. |
| Traffic | Compare normal traffic with an unobstructed prescribed path **at matched path length**. Record blocked seconds and speed lost to traffic. |
| Kick | From identical three-quarter states, vary late-speed ability; separately preserve the closer’s actual deficit. Measure catchable distance, not just wins. |
| Energy | At fixed position/speed/path, transplant leader versus closer energy. Record when the energy ceiling binds and energy remaining at the finish. |

**H:** closers may be surrendering more distance than their saved energy can recover, with traffic adding another penalty. These tests distinguish that from insufficient kick or an ineffective timing instruction. Do not add a free “closer bonus.”

**Gate correction:** the shelter gate requires **energy gain >2**, while the log says 3. Freeze one version. More importantly, that arbitrary energy margin can reject a configuration with a genuine performance benefit. Keep a controlled physical-saving test and a paired performance test with uncertainty; don’t require the same numerical saving across different cost scales.

## 3. Replay: physical continuity, not mandatory race shapes

Change two proposed gates:

- **“Every pass is an outside move” is wrong.** Passing inside an available opening, or while already alongside, can be valid. Require a continuously clear path.
- Losing relative position needs no special event: another horse can simply travel faster. Require the displacement to follow recorded velocities—not an invented “tiring” label.

Add hard checks for:
- Swept horse/sulky footprints during lane changes, including simultaneous merges.
- Track boundaries and physically valid starting width/rows for each field size.
- Bounded lateral speed/acceleration, longitudinal acceleration/braking, and no position snapbacks from traffic correction.
- Consistent turn-path distance, finish crossings and post-finish motion.

Treat field spread and frequency of wide trips as **provisional plausibility distributions**, not universal hard bounds that erase legitimate unusual races.

**Record:** engine/config/track versions; stable horse IDs; initial conditions; timestamped longitudinal and lateral position, velocity and heading; maneuver start/end; break/recovery and obstruction intervals; exact sectional and finish crossings. Keep energy, targets and constraint reasons in a diagnostic stream.

Store authoritative physics-step trajectories initially. A 4 Hz replay export is acceptable only after interpolation is shown to preserve paths, clearances and finish order. Smooth lane changes must exist **in the simulation**, not be cosmetic animation over instantaneous jumps.

## 4. Launch plan

The fresh-world approach is correct. I would add:

- Pair candidates on **foundation stock and world seeds**, applying rules before history begins. Test history lengths such as 10/25/50 seasons; inspect founder contributions, relatedness, usable nick diversity and economy at Year 0—not merely sire concentration.
- Report starter-barn fairness and early/late entrant outcomes separately; include higher informed-player participation.
- Freeze and archive pilot builds. Log Patrick’s information, decisions and observations before showing hidden outcomes; he is a valuable pilot, not a population-level sample.
- Keep breeding/economy launch tests explicitly provisional until the race engine is settled: changing which traits win can change selection and drift.

**Priority:** closer-mechanism assay and continuous lateral geometry first; no broad refit until those are understood.
