## 1. Breeding: accept the result, narrow the headline

**Measured:** open eligibility plus quality-blind AI substantially reduces late drift versus reset, with much lower sire concentration. Open eligibility alone meets the reported noninferiority criteria. Neither result establishes stationarity.

Three qualifications for Patrick’s briefing:

- **“Beats reset” means on drift and concentration—not overall gameplay.** The weaker herd and potentially easier competition are central tradeoffs.
- **H5 is an eligibility × mating-policy interaction**, not simply “eligibility is the main engine.” Open eligibility with quality-weighted AI still has approximately reset’s genetic drift.
- **Edge preservation currently passes by point estimate only.** Before calling the full criterion satisfied, calculate the paired confidence bound for  
  `candidate edge − 0.8 × reset edge`.  
  Its lower bound should be nonnegative. Also confirm whether this is the matched-mare/access assay or the original confounded strategy-barn comparison.

One important update: reset’s earlier drift advantage is **not clearly sustained over seasons 50–100**—`pkg` and reset now have similar mean frozen slopes. Present both time windows, not only the favorable earlier result.

### Only decision-changing follow-ups

**One targeted comparison:** reset, open/0.6, open/**0.3**, open/0.0, using paired histories and the existing criteria. Add these measurements to that run:

1. **Competitive strength and exploitation.** Give equal starting assets to ordinary and informed **public-information** player policies; keep the oracle as a ceiling. Measure absolute foal quality, race win/prize share and progression against AI. Include a higher informed-player share:  
   **H:** quality-blind AI may stabilize an AI-dominated population but lose that benefit when more matings select strongly for quality.
2. **Stud economics and access.** Record fees actually paid, covers sold, sire-owner income distribution, cash/reserve failures, unfilled breeding attempts and birth counts. Separate incumbent top-sire owners from newly eligible owners.
3. **Absolute strength, not just the edge.** Report player and AI performance levels separately. A preserved difference can coexist with both groups becoming weaker—or players becoming dominant.

**H:** open/0.3 may offer the best compromise, but the endpoints do not establish that. Patrick can receive the current choice now, marked with these unresolved consequences.

## 2. Race: yes to a small fit, conditional on hard gates

The mechanism evidence now justifies a **limited calibration search**, not another broad mechanism redesign. DT **1/16** is a reasonable working resolution, not a certified final one.

### Hard acceptance gates

- **Geometry:** zero illegal overlaps, pass-throughs or lane-change conflicts in adversarial tests; valid finish interpolation and ordering. The previously incomplete crossing detector must cover lane changes. A fitter cannot waive this.
- **Numerical robustness:** finalists rerun at 1/32. Inspect the five existing winner flips with margins ≥0.1 seconds. Predeclare time-error and outcome-tolerance limits; reject unexplained large-margin flips or tactical conclusions that reverse with DT.
- **Energy and shelter:** retain the deterministic energy-dose response and controlled shelter saving; verify the saving produces a performance benefit in the unobstructed release test.
- **Boxing:** retain the forced-box delay, no crossing, and prompt release when room opens. Test more than one blockage duration and location; don’t require exactly 0.46 seconds.
- **Pace response:** fixed horses under scripted slow/moderate/fast openings must show continuous gap response and paid exertion, without free catch-up. The current early-speed sweep changes ability as well as pace, so it is not sufficient alone.
- **Basic capability checks:** graded class advantages and gait/break response must remain sensible. Do not enforce agreement with the old weighted rating formula.

**Keep style balance and race-time bands as soft, provisional targets.** Do not require every duel to hurt the leader. The dig-in ablation identifies a parameter-mediated effect; it does not prove that responding to pressure should always be disadvantageous.

### Held-out validation

Use untouched seeds and fields, half-mile tracks, small and large fields, post rotations, zero/one/multiple leavers, strong and weak horses, varied stamina, and breaks enabled. Reserve several move timings and pace profiles from fitting.

Screen at 1/8 if necessary, but **rank finalists and accept only at 1/16**, with the 1/32 robustness check. Freeze the test bank and gates before searching; report failed gates separately from aggregate loss.
