## C. Race: one bounded mechanism round, then conditional fitting

First, two interpretation corrections:

- **Energy is not simply stranded above 45.** Your own dose test shows 75 beats 45 by **0.78 seconds**: surplus energy delays falling below the threshold. “No instantaneous extra top speed” is correct; “energy they cannot use” is not.
- **Convergence is improving, not certified.** Twelve percent winner disagreement at 1/8 versus 1/16 remains material. Compare 1/16 with 1/32, separating near-tie flips from substantial margin changes. Also, the attached default remains `DT = 0.5`; require an explicit tested DT.

### Minimal pace response — hypothesis to test

Replace the six-length tracking cutoff with a **continuous, bounded gap controller**:

- Track a tactically relevant horse ahead, including when it is beyond six lengths.
- Desired speed = smoothed reference speed plus a capped correction for excess gap.
- Limit that request by the horse’s existing physical ceiling and a sustainable-effort policy; charge normal energy costs.
- Permit deliberately conceding ground. No forced catch-up, leader slowdown or free speed.

Start with one shared controller, not separate style bonuses. Test prescribed slow/moderate/fast openings: realized gaps, exertion and remaining energy should respond continuously across the old six-length boundary. **Do not require every hot pace to lose**; require the field to react plausibly and pay for chasing.

### Forced-box control

Use scripted, identical leader and outside-blocker trajectories in both arms, independent of the subject’s actions:

- Blocked arm: outside exit unavailable for a fixed interval.
- Open arm: disable only that blocker’s collision constraint for the subject.
- Disable breaks and shelter in both; hold initial state and instructions fixed.

Assert that the subject requests acceleration, is physically restrained only in the blocked arm, cannot cross either obstacle, and loses distance during confinement. Measure immediate delay before assessing finish time. Count boxed time once per step.

### Fit gate

After those tests—and lane-change collision assertions—pass, start a **small constrained fit** with fixed scenario banks and held-out validation. Gates should cover geometry, DT robustness, energy response, shelter benefit and the isolated box delay. Use verified tactical scenarios, not unconditional “duel bad/late move good” rules.

Do not let style balance compensate for a failed mechanics gate.
