## 1. Honest clock: keep 0.35 provisionally, not as a validated optimum

**Measured:** photos are plausible at 9–10%; median margins of 2.8–3.4 L exceed the old under-2-L target. Record that as an intentional retake decision, not a pass. The 0.60-second figure spread is not evidence of improved accuracy by itself: squeezing seconds also squeezes errors and tier spacing.

**Single measurement:** **adjacent-tier win probability in standardized, matched fields**, using pre-race badges, balanced posts and fresh randomness. Report it separately for each tier boundary. That tells whether a displayed class step represents a meaningful competitive advantage rather than merely different clock labels.

Crucially, a common affine squeeze changes neither ranking nor that probability. It cannot repair poor class separation; it only changes the displayed spread and margins. If the separation is satisfactory, **leave 0.35 for Patrick’s visual judgment**. There is no measured optimum here.

Also verify whether fixed thresholds—one-length fights, minimum noses—make the new engine more than a simple affine rescaling.
