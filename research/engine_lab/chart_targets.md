## Provisional bands for the lab (8-horse fields)

- Mile track: post 1 wins about 15–20%, post 8 about 6–10%, with a gentle slope. These figures come from a five-eighths-mile track, so they are soft targets, not gates (review 11).
- Half-mile track: post 1 wins about 19–27%, post 8 about 1–6%. The slope should be steep.
- Winner on or within a few lengths of the lead at the half: most races on any size of track, more on a half-mile.
- The leader at the half from post 7–8 should be clearly rarer than from post 1–2, and rarer still on a half-mile.
