# The retake: walkthrough for the final review

Open the candidate at http://127.0.0.1:8113 (the live world copied down, moved onto the honest clock, and run forward
twelve race nights with every player barn's fit horses entered; signed in as your barn; never saving) beside
trotdynasty.com. Same horses, same barns. Five things changed, in the order you'll meet them.

## 1. The honest clock

Every horse runs its own time. On live, a beaten horse's time was the winner's class plus its margin; here it is what
the horse itself ran that night, on that trip, in that form. The breed's spread pulled in with it: an S+ mile is about
1:51.5, a C mile about 1:54.8, and a bar is worth the same at the wire as on the clock, about four lengths. Every saved
time, record and replay moved onto the same clock, so history still reads true (Northern Star's marks included).

Look at: any Results page (margins and times agree), a horse's lifetime mark against its letter, the track records.

## 2. Every bar races

Manners now get a horse away clean, or leave it fractious behind the car (it hops leaving, the caption says so, the
trip note says "fractious at the start", and it has ground to make up). Grit now wins the head-and-heads: two horses
within a length at the wire fight it out and the grittier one gets the bob ("fought it out at the wire"). Speed and
Stamina as before. Measured on 1,500 random fields: Manners and Grit each move finishing places about a quarter more
than before.

Look at: trip notes on Results; replays with "fractious behind the car", "go head and head", "fight it out",
"is backing up" and "breaks stride" captions, and the hop at the gate or where a horse broke.

## 3. The letter, read off the clock

A proven horse (five starts) wears the median of its last ten miles, read fairly for the trips it got; the line under
its name says so and names the next line up: "A on its last 10 miles, 1:53.1 read fairly · S at 1:52.2 or better".
Unproven horses wear a hollow UNPROVEN letter from their bars against horses their age. Retired horses keep their
prime. A horse on the way down loses its letter the same way it earned it. No hidden rating makes a letter anywhere.

Look at: your own horses on both sites; Saga Spell (10, in decline); the Compendium entry "The clock and the letter".

## 4. The replay speaks

One tell per bar: the fractious start (Manners), the break (Manners), the duel and the fight at the wire (Grit), backing
up off the turn (Stamina), the run from the back (Speed). The choreographer draws the positions as live does; the
captions name what it drew, when it happened.

## 5. Breeding you can read

On a mare's Breed page each stallion's outlook band now shows the letter the foal would likely wear at its peak, on the
same ruler as every badge (S+ included), with the spread in words; under each stallion, his produce record: what his
foals wear. The gene-revert chance is the launch rule (half of live's), so lines hold.

Look at: Breeding → a broodmare → Breed.

## What didn't change

The art, the pages, the bars, the card, the program's layout, training, the market, the stakes book. This retake is in
what the game *means*, read off one engine, not in how it looks.
