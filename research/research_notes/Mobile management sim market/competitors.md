# Competitive set: horse-racing stable-management games and mobile sports managers (2024–2026)

Scope note: figures labelled "store" come from App Store / Google Play listings; "estimate" comes from Sensor Tower, Apptopia, Marlvel, AppGoblin or similar third-party trackers, which are weekly/point-in-time snapshots and not audited. Where no 2025–2026 data existed, the latest available figure is given with its date.

## Who are the direct horse-racing sim competitors and how big are they?

### Takeaway
Harness racing has exactly one mobile competitor (Off and Pacing) and it is small (a few hundred to ~1K store ratings, coin-pack IAP, last update a billing fix in July 2025). The thoroughbred side is led by Rival Stars Horse Racing (PikPok, 50M+ lifetime downloads, ~$50–75K peak weekly US revenue) and Horse Racing Manager (Third Time, 6.4K iOS ratings); Photo Finish LIVE is a crypto-backed 24/7 racing game with $30M+ in entry fees spent since 2023; the old web sims (SIMHorseRacing.com, since 1998) are free and still running.

### Cited Findings

**Off and Pacing: Horse Racing (The Farm Ventures / Ryan Clements, Ontario) — the only harness-racing sim**
- Free with IAP; iOS 4.1/5 from 148 ratings (store); coin packs $0.99 (50K coins) to $54.99 (5M coins); last update v2.18 on July 14 2025 described as a billing update — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/off-and-pacing-horse-racing/id1185495888)
- Features: 25 real tracks, 250+ real drivers, four regions (CA/AU/NZ/US), live 3D races, stakes, breeding retired mares to studs, auctions and claiming races — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/off-and-pacing-horse-racing/id1185495888)
- Third-party estimate (May 2026): 3.8/5 from 936 reviews, "polarized" reception, concerns on game balance and technical stability — [Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/com-offandpacing-www); Google Play aggregator lists 3.7/5 from 785 reviews — [grand-screen](https://grand-screen.com/games/off-and-pacing-horse-racing/)
- Review excerpts: Aug 2025 "fun but repetitive", asks for feed choices, more training options, driving without a Pro license, and no races between 1 and 7 AM (implies races are scheduled around the clock); 2018 "Rigged" review claiming draws/outcomes follow a pattern, developer replied draws are random; 2019 reviews praise claiming/buying/breeding mix and the Discord community, and that devs police cheating — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/off-and-pacing-horse-racing/id1185495888)
- Industry press covered it in 2017 as "drawing new fans" to harness racing — [Harness Racing Update](https://harnessracingupdate.com/2017/07/07/lines-off-pacing-app-drawing-new-fans/); first Australian track (Melton) added — [Harnesslink](https://harnesslink.com/australia/melton-first-aussie-track-off-and-pacing/)

**Rival Stars Horse Racing (PikPok, NZ) — the category leader**
- Free-to-play with IAP on mobile; 50M+ downloads across mobile and PC VR; Steam 4,600+ reviews at 92% positive — [PikPok VR pre-order release](https://pikpok.com/news/rival-stars-horse-racing-vr-edition-opens-pre-orders-on-january-20-2025/)
- Sensor Tower US iOS Q2 2025 (estimate): #1 horse game by revenue, weekly peak ~$52.9K early May, downloads 4.8K/week late April, weekly active users 33.9K→44.4K — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-ios-top-5-horse games-revenue-us-60a7063328293296043b4e31); US unified Q1 2025 weekly peak ~$76K — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-unified-top-5-horse%20games-units-us-60a7063328293296043b4e31)
- Steam estimate: ~152.9K copies, ~$2.8M gross (medium confidence) — [raijin.gg](https://raijin.gg/app/1166860/Rival_Stars_Horse_Racing_Desktop_Edition)
- iOS has 9 IAPs — [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1215477266)
- Still expanding: VR Edition (Quest 3 / SteamVR, Jan 2025) — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/rival-stars-horse-racing-vr-edition-out-now-on-meta-quest-3-and-steamvr); PS5/Xbox 28 April 2026, Switch 28 May 2026 — [rivalstarshorseracing.com](https://www.rivalstarshorseracing.com/post/rival-stars-horse-racing-launches-on-consoles-today)
- Metacritic critic summary: easy to pick up, pretty, "rarely feels engaging where it counts" — [Metacritic](https://legacy.prod.metacritic.com/game/switch/rival-stars-horse-racing/critic-reviews)

**Horse Racing Manager 2025/2026 (Third Time, Inc. — same studio as Photo Finish LIVE)**
- Free with IAP; iOS 4.6/5 from 6.4K ratings (store); ten "HRM Pack" IAPs from $0.99 to $99.99; live PVP races "every 5 minutes"; feeding/rest/training, breeding with rare genetic traits, jockey/horse cosmetics — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1275199710)
- Third-party estimate: 4.43/5 from 43.2K reviews (likely Google Play), polarized — [Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/games/horse-racing-manager-2026)
- Complaints: horseshoes (premium currency) needed to feed horses and keep extra horses racing, recurring cost; "whole game revolves around earning cash"; one buyer of horseshoe packs got no SS horse across several tries, "I hate being ripped off"; cash becomes useless for established players, want in-stable breeding paid in cash; slow support; alleged cheating — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1275199710); [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1275199710/horse-racing-manager-2024/reviews)

**Photo Finish LIVE (Third Time Games) — blockchain, 24/7 racing**
- Virtual horse racing on Solana; $CROWN and $DERBY tokens; players can own and stake tracks — [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/projects/photo-finish); [DappRadar](https://dappradar.com/dapp/photo-finish-live)
- Since launching in 2023, players have spent over $30 million on race entry fees (developer claim, Sept 2025) — [Third Time Games](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=7396)
- Official Kentucky Derby gaming partner since 2023; races run 24/7; monthly Virtual Kentucky Derby; "thousands" of stable owners; free starter horse; mobile app "coming soon" as of July 2024 — [KentuckyDerby.com](https://www.kentuckyderby.com/horses/news/photo-finish-live-unlocks-a-new-generation-of-horse-racing-fans/)
- Claiming races, trainer horses, major races with 4 undercards paid in Crown, weekly real-race Pick'Em contest on Saturdays — [Third Time Games](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=4514); [Pick'Em](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=7982)
- Solana Derby interactive spectating/betting event at Breakpoint Singapore, Sept 21 2025 — [Third Time Games](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=7396)

**Equestrian the Game (Kavalri Games, Sweden) — riding/showjumping, not racing, but same "adult horse game" audience**
- Free; iOS 4.0/5 from 10.0K reviews (store); 7 IAPs $1.49–$99.99 — [App Store MX](https://apps.apple.com/MX/app/id1468871996); [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1468871996)
- Sensor Tower Q1 2025 (estimate): Europe weekly revenue fell from $23.5K to $10.3K over the quarter; US peak $12.2K early quarter with falling downloads — [Sensor Tower Europe](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-unified-top-5-horse%20games-revenue-europe-60a7063328293296043b4e31); [Sensor Tower US](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q1-ios-top-5-horse games-revenue-us-60a7063328293296043b4e31)
- A player review calls it "Pay to win" — [game-solver](https://game-solver.com/equestrian-the-game/); studio retrospective Oct 2024 — [The Mane Quest](https://www.themanequest.com/blog/2024/10/16/weve-learned-a-lot-but-our-mission-to-make-great-horse-games-hasnt-changed-reflecting-on-equestrian-the-game-with-kavalri-games-molly-ericson)

**Star Stable Online (Star Stable Entertainment, Sweden) — MMO riding, subscription**

**Rest of the US iOS top-5 "horse games" by revenue, Q2 2025 (estimate)**: Horse Riding Tales (AppForge) $18.6K peak; Star Equestrian (Foxie) $12.4K; My Little Pony World (Budge) $6.9K — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-ios-top-5-horse games-revenue-us-60a7063328293296043b4e31). **Derby Owners Club lineage**
- Sega NAOMI arcade (1999), card-based horse persistence; PC port 2004 shut 2006; arcade service ended March 31 2017 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derby_Owners_Club); [Sega Retro](https://segaretro.org/Derby_Owners_Club)

**Web sims**
- SIMHorseRacing.com: free since 1998, "no purchase necessary — ever", 4 surfaces, 16-week game year, new players get one game year on a level playing field before moving to a Junior circuit — [simhorseracing.com](https://simhorseracing.com/); [rules](https://simhorseracing.com/rules.php); still covered by racing press for its Triple Crown season — [Racing Dudes](https://racingdudes.com/triple-crown-season-underway-at-simhorseracing-com/)

### Inferences
- Off and Pacing is small enough (under 1K ratings per store) that Trot Dynasty's realistic ceiling in harness is the same niche audience; the game has had no feature update visible since mid-2025, so it is effectively in maintenance.
- The thoroughbred leader (Rival Stars) monetizes at tens of thousands of dollars per week in the US alone; management-first titles (HRM) monetize through consumable premium currency tied to upkeep, which is exactly what their reviews resent.
- Photo Finish LIVE shows that a 24/7 scheduled racing loop with real stakes attracts heavy spending ($30M in entries), but its crypto dependency keeps it out of the mainstream app stores.

### Gaps
- No full-year 2025 download or revenue totals for any title; Sensor Tower pages give only weekly peaks per market.
- "Hoofbeat" returned no results under that name; it may be a defunct or renamed web sim.
- No Discord member counts found for any game; Off and Pacing's Discord is mentioned in reviews but not sized.
- Photo Finish LIVE player counts are not disclosed beyond "thousands"; the "mobile app coming soon" (July 2024) status was not confirmed.

## What do sports-manager mobile games charge, and what models do the successful ones use?

### Takeaway
Three models coexist: free + consumable IAP/ads at mass scale (Top Eleven, Football Manager Mobile), a cheap premium unlock on a free base (Retro Bowl, ~$1–1.39 "Unlimited"), and old-style paid apps (Motorsport Manager Mobile 3 at $1.99–$7.99 plus small IAPs). The licensed-baseball cash machine (MLB Tap Sports) reached $340M lifetime bookings but has since been shut down.

### Cited Findings
- Top Eleven (Nordeus, owned by Take-Two since Feb 2021 at a $378M valuation): free; 300M+ registered users (Sept 2025 report) vs 240M+ per another source; Bundesliga league partnership for 2025/26; AppGoblin iOS estimate ~$10K+/month split 29% IAP / 71% ads (rough) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordeus); [PocketGamer.biz 220M downloads](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/73335/top-eleven-220-million-downloads/); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/459035295)
- Football Manager Mobile: free download with 10 IAPs from $0.99 to $17.99; FM25 Mobile was distributed through Netflix — [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/app/apple/1434524349); [Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/football-manager-2025-mobile/)
- Motorsport Manager Mobile 3 (Playsport, 2018): paid, $1.99 on US App Store with 8 IAPs $1.99–$9.99; €7.99 iOS / €6.99 Android cited in a 2025 German roundup; still listed in 2025, no discontinuation found — [App Pricing Lab](https://www.apppricinglab.com/app/apple/1346580540); [futurezone.at](https://futurezone.at/games/ios-android-beste-apps-games-management-manager-spiele-simulatoion-fussball-formel-1-metro/402546584)
- Retro Bowl (New Star Games): free, no ads, features behind a $1.39 "Unlimited" purchase (2020 price); current IAPs $0.99–$29.99 (coaching credits); 7M US downloads with zero UA spend; "tens of millions of players" without an NFL license — [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2020/09/27/retro-bowl-football-game-review/); [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1478902583); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/uk-based-new-star-games-takes-us-by-storm-with-retro-bowl); [Awful Announcing](https://awfulannouncing.com/gaming/while-ea-fights-over-nfl-licenses-retro-bowl-is-winning-football-gaming-without-one.html)
- MLB Tap Sports Baseball (Glu/EA): $340M lifetime bookings by 2020 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mlb-tap-sports-baseball-growing-strong); reported shut down March 2023 (low-quality source, unverified) — [sportssurge](https://sportssurge.alibaba.com/baseball/what-happened-to-tap-sports-baseball)
- US mobile sports game revenue $648.8M for the 12 months to May 2021 (+16% YoY, Sensor Tower) — [wnhub](https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-18818)
- Horse-specific IAP ladders: Off and Pacing $0.99–$54.99 coin packs; HRM $0.99–$99.99; Equestrian $1.49–$99.99 (store, above)

### Inferences
- Successful management titles rarely charge up front; the two that do (Motorsport Manager Mobile, Retro Bowl's unlock) sit at $1–8 and sell on fairness/no-ads.
- $99.99 top-tier packs are standard in horse F2P titles but correlate with the pay-to-win complaints below.

### Gaps
- No 2025 revenue disclosure for Nordeus, Football Manager Mobile, Retro Bowl or any NBA manager title; no NBA-manager mobile data surfaced at all.
- Motorsport Manager Mobile pricing varies by region/date and the 2026 status is unconfirmed.

## What do reviews say players want and hate?

### Takeaway
Praise clusters on depth (breeding, claiming, auctions), community, and real-world tracks/names; complaints cluster on premium currency tied to upkeep, suspected rigging, repetitive loops, thin training/feeding options, slow support and unaddressed cheating.

### Cited Findings
- Off and Pacing: "fun but repetitive"; wants feed choices, more training options, driving without Pro license, no overnight races; "Rigged" accusation on draws; praise for claiming/buying/breeding mix and community — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/off-and-pacing-horse-racing/id1185495888); third-party summary flags balance and stability concerns — [Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/com-offandpacing-www)
- Horse Racing Manager: premium horseshoes required to feed/keep horses racing; paid packs that failed to deliver promised SS horses ("ripped off"); cash useless late-game; slow support; cheating complaints — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1275199710)
- Rival Stars: breeding boosters/diet plans "hard to get", "no easy way of getting them" — [Pocket Gamer breeding guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/rival-stars-horse-racing/breeding-guide); critics: not engaging beyond horse fans — [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/rival-stars-horse-racing); Steam users 92% positive — [PikPok](https://pikpok.com/news/rival-stars-horse-racing-vr-edition-opens-pre-orders-on-january-20-2025/)
- Equestrian the Game: "Pay to win" user comment — [game-solver](https://game-solver.com/equestrian-the-game/)
- Retro Bowl praised for free, ad-free, one-time unlock model — [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2020/09/27/retro-bowl-football-game-review/)

### Inferences
- "Rigged" is a recurring accusation in any sim where outcomes are hidden; visible, explainable race math (Photo Finish publishes a "Race Math" post — [Third Time](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=4328)) is a differentiator.
- Upkeep paid in premium currency is the single most-hated pattern; cosmetics-only spending avoids it.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads surfaced for any horse title (searches returned unrelated content); r/horseracing and game subreddits were not directly readable.
- Review excerpts are a handful of store reviews each, not systematic sentiment analysis.

## Which games have a nightly or scheduled live-race loop, and how do players respond?

### Takeaway
Photo Finish LIVE (24/7 continuous races plus a monthly Virtual Kentucky Derby and weekly Saturday pick'em), Horse Racing Manager (live PVP every 5 minutes) and Off and Pacing (scheduled real-clock races, including overnight) all run clock-based racing; SIMHorseRacing runs a 16-week game year. None found runs a single nightly card; players of Off and Pacing explicitly ask for no 1–7 AM races.

### Cited Findings
- Photo Finish LIVE: races 24/7, monthly Virtual Kentucky Derby (14th running by July 2024), winner's race "went off at midnight UK time" — [KentuckyDerby.com](https://www.kentuckyderby.com/horses/news/photo-finish-live-unlocks-a-new-generation-of-horse-racing-fans/); Saturday Pick'Em on real races with daily and seasonal prizes — [Third Time](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=7982); $30M+ entry fees since 2023 — [Third Time](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=7396)
- Horse Racing Manager: live PVP races every 5 minutes — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1275199710)
- Off and Pacing: reviewer asks for no races between 1 and 7 AM, implying real-clock scheduling around the clock — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/off-and-pacing-horse-racing/id1185495888)
- SIMHorseRacing: 16-week game year, Triple Crown season covered by press — [simhorseracing.com](https://www.simhorseracing.com/new/); [Racing Dudes](https://racingdudes.com/triple-crown-season-underway-at-simhorseracing-com/)

### Inferences
- Scheduled real-clock racing is accepted when it is frequent; the complaint is about races the owner cannot watch. A single fixed evening card with a day-ahead program is unoccupied space.
- The pick'em layer (Photo Finish, weekly, prizes) has precedent and is well received enough to run as a series.

### Gaps
- No direct player-sentiment data on the 24/7 loop beyond the developer's own posts; no retention figures.

## Market gaps left open (synthesis)

- Fairness/transparency: "rigged" accusations in Off and Pacing and HRM reviews; only Photo Finish publishes its race math — [Third Time](https://thirdtimegames.com/?p=4328).
- Nightly live card: no competitor runs one fixed evening card; existing loops are 24/7 or every-5-minutes.
- Breeding depth: praised wherever present (Off and Pacing, Rival Stars, Photo Finish), but gated by scarce boosters (Rival Stars) or premium currency (HRM).
- Cosmetic-only economy: every horse F2P title sells currency/horses/upkeep; none found sells cosmetics only. Retro Bowl's one-time unlock is the closest "fair" model and is widely praised.
- Web-first play: only the 1998-era SIMHorseRacing and crypto-native Photo Finish are web-first; neither is a modern mobile-friendly web app.
- Harness specifically: one competitor, in maintenance since July 2025, with sub-1K store ratings.
