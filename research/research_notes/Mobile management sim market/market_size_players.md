# Mobile management / tycoon simulation market, 2025-2026: size, growth, players

Scope note: "management sim" is not a reporting category at Sensor Tower, GameAnalytics or Newzoo. The closest tracked buckets are Sensor Tower's **Simulation** genre (which lumps idle/tycoon/farm/life sims), the **Sports Manager** subgenre inside Sports, and **Strategy** (4X, which shares the resource-optimization audience). Figures below are tagged with which bucket they measure.

## How big is the mobile simulation / management / tycoon genre, and how is it trending?

### Takeaway
Simulation is the largest or second-largest mobile genre by *downloads* (~9.8B installs in 2024, ~6% of all game installs) but no public source gives its 2025 revenue; the whole mobile market was flat in 2025 (IAP $81.75B, +1.3%; downloads 50.4B, -7.2%) while Strategy ($20.2B) was the only genre growing on revenue, downloads and time spent. Simulators were one of the three biggest contributors to time-spent growth in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Mobile games 2025: 50.41B downloads (-7.2% YoY, decline accelerating), $81.75B IAP revenue (+1.3% YoY, third straight year of growth), time spent +0.9% YoY (US and Japan recovered after a 2024 dip; China declining) — [Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026 via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026); confirmed in [Sensor Tower press release](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-52-billion-downloads-82b-iap-revenue-on-mobile-and-12b-premium-revenue-on-steam)
- 2025 revenue by store: App Store $52.5B (+0.6%), Google Play $30B (+2.8%). Installs: App Store 7.8B (-5.7%), Google Play 42.4B (-7.3%). Share of installs: Google Play 81%, App Store 15%, PC+console 4% — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Strategy was the largest mobile segment at $20.2B in 2025 and the only genre to gain on revenue, downloads and time spent (led by Last War, Whiteout Survival). Puzzle $14.4B. "Simulators, puzzles, strategy" were the largest contributors to time-spent growth; no simulation revenue figure published — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Hybrid-casual IAP revenue +20% YoY in 2025, the only segment with meaningful revenue growth; hypercasual the only segment with download growth — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Regional supply-side shift 2025: Asian studios +$2.58B IAP, European +$0.36B, North American studios -$1.78B — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Simulation genre 2024 (older figure): 9.8B installs, +0.4% YoY, 6.17% of all mobile game downloads, second among subgenres behind platformers/runners (8.34%); Sensor Tower calls simulators a download market leader. No revenue figure given — [Sensor Tower Mobile Market in 2025 via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-market-in-2025)
- A search-snippet summary of a Sensor Tower table put Simulation 2024 IAP revenue at +8.8% YoY; the table was garbled and not verified — [Sensor Tower State of Mobile Gaming 2025 PDF (unverified table)](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/sensor_tower__state_of_mobile_gaming_2025__en.pdf)
- US mobile games 2024 (older): revenue +9%, downloads -7% — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-market-in-2025)
- Sports genre 2024 (older): revenue -6.3%; 85% of downloads went to games older than 2 years — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-mobile-market-in-2025)
- Sports Manager subgenre, US, Jun 2020-May 2021 (old, but the only public subgenre number): ~$12M revenue, +60.4% YoY, the fastest-growing sports subgenre; 3.7M downloads (+17.8%); examples Top Eleven, F1 Clash. Whole US sports genre: $648.8M (+16%), 129.3M downloads (-16.3%), #10 genre by US spend — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/us-mobile-sports-games-650-million-revenue)
- Sensor Tower's genre page states sports manager games have increased net revenue despite a multi-year decline in download volume, "a shift toward high-value monetization" (years not specified in snippet) — [Sensor Tower search summary](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-android-top-5-sports%20games-revenue-nl-6058d8b0241bc16eb8555111)
- Top Eleven passed 220M lifetime downloads (2020, old) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/top-eleven-220-million-downloads/); Netherlands Android weekly revenue peaked at ~$14.7K in late May 2025, OSM 25/26 ~$13.6K in late June 2025 — [Sensor Tower Q2 2025 NL chart](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-android-top-5-sports%20games-revenue-nl-6058d8b0241bc16eb8555111)
- Rival Stars Horse Racing (PikPok; breed/train/manage/jockey) passed 50M downloads across mobile and PC VR per the 2026 console announcement; it is F2P with IAP $0.99-$9.99 on mobile — [COGconnected](https://cogconnected.com/2026/03/rival-stars-horse-racing-is-heading-to-consoles-on-28-april-2026/); [PikPok](https://pikpok.com/news/rival-stars-horse-racing-available-now/)
- Newzoo's 2025 mobile total is much higher than Sensor Tower's and inconsistent across its own updates: $108B (+7.7%) in a Dec 2025 update vs $113.3B (+10.7%) in a later summary, total games market $201.6B (+9.1%). Newzoo counts gross consumer spend incl. ads/web shops and China differently; do not mix with Sensor Tower's net IAP — [Newzoo via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-gaming-market-surpassed-200); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/newzoo-revises-2025-global-games-market-growth-forecast-to-1889bn)

### Inferences
- Management/tycoon is a high-volume, low-ARPU corner: Simulation ranks top-2 in installs but is never named among the top revenue genres, so per-install revenue is well below Strategy/Puzzle. A niche manager title should plan on modest ARPDAU and long tenure rather than whale spend.
- The sports manager subgenre is tiny in dollar terms (tens of millions USD/yr in the US as of 2021) but was growing fastest on both installs and revenue, and Sensor Tower's later note says it kept raising revenue while installs shrank, i.e. a small, deepening audience.
- Trends favor the format: time spent is growing in simulators, and the market's only growth pockets are hybrid-casual (idle/tycoon mechanics plus IAP) rather than pure ad-funded hypercasual.
- North American studios lost $1.78B of IAP in 2025 while Asian studios gained; US-made niche games face a shrinking home-supply market but US consumer spend itself was still growing (+9% in 2024).

### Gaps
- No public 2025 revenue or download figure for the Simulation genre, nor for idle/tycoon, farm/stable management, or sports manager subgenres; Sensor Tower, AppMagic and data.ai keep those behind paywalls. The AppQuantum/AppMagic idle-tycoon overview (2022) has no numbers on its public page.
- No horse-racing/stable-management market sizing exists publicly; Rival Stars is the only datapoint.

## Who plays management sims on mobile, and how does that differ from casual games?

### Takeaway
Tycoon/crafting-management players skew female (~61% in GameRefinery's 2022 snapshot) and 25-44, which is close to the overall mobile audience (roughly 50/50 gender, average age ~36), whereas 4X strategy and sports games skew heavily male (76-90%+). Casual/puzzle audiences are older (60% of US casual players are over 35) and more female; Android carries ~81% of installs but the App Store takes ~64% of mobile game revenue.

### Cited Findings
- GameRefinery (Feb 2022, older): women are 61% of the audience for resource-optimization mechanics in tycoon and crafting games; men are 76-84% of 4X strategy and synchronous PvP audiences; main age band for both 4X and tycoon/crafting is 25-44; tycoon players prefer the more casual titles, 4X players the more complex ones; both groups share a motivation to optimize resources — [GameRefinery via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamerefinery-users-motivations-in); percentages via [GameIndustryLibrary summary](https://gameindustrylibrary.com/documents/motivation-and-demographics-snapshot-report-2022/read)
- US 2025 (Sensor Tower): 75% of PC/console players are under 35; 60% of casual and hypercasual players are over 35; women over-index in lifestyle, puzzle, casino; men dominate sports, strategy, shooters (no percentages) — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- AppLovin/Kantar survey of 2,500 US adults who played a mobile game in the past 3 months (Feb 2026): 52% female, 48% male — [AppLovin](https://www.metaply.ai/applovin/a1-who-plays-mobile-games)
- ESA 2025: women 47% of US gamers (46% prior year); average US gamer age 36; 60% of US adults play weekly — [GameLuster summary of ESA](https://gameluster.com/?p=122574)
- Business of Apps (2024 data): action, strategy, racing and sports played mostly by men; word, trivia, board, educational split evenly; puzzle/word/board skew older — [Business of Apps](https://businessofapps.com/?p=97879)
- One ad-network source claims strategy/action/racing/sports are "+90% male" (ad-network device data, weaker source) — [maf.ad](https://maf.ad/en/blog/mobile-gamers-demographics/)
- Mintel US Mobile Gaming 2025: middle-aged mobile gamers are the biggest spenders; Android reaches across income levels, iOS leans premium (percentages paywalled) — [Mintel](https://store.mintel.com/report/us-mobile-gaming-market-report)
- Platform split, global 2025: Google Play 81% of installs vs App Store 15%; revenue App Store $52.5B vs Google Play $30B (~64/36) — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Retention is highest in North America, Europe and Oceania; Europe has the longest sessions — [GameAnalytics 2026 via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)

### Inferences
- A stable/racing *management* game sits at the intersection of two audiences: the female-leaning 25-44 tycoon/crafting cohort (management, breeding, decoration) and the male-leaning sports/strategy cohort (racing, competition). Which it attracts depends on presentation more than mechanics.
- Versus casual: management players are younger (25-44 vs 35+), more gender-balanced, and motivated by optimization rather than relaxation/decoration, though decoration mechanics appear in every top-100 US casual game and are a proven hook for the female cohort.
- For a US/English-first product, iOS is the money platform (about 2/3 of revenue on ~1/6 of installs) and Android the reach platform.

### Gaps
- No 2024+ gender/age data specific to tycoon, management or sports-manager mobile games; the GameRefinery figures are from 2022.
- No region split for the Simulation genre; no US-only iOS/Android split for simulation players (Mintel/Statista have it, paywalled).

## What are typical D1/D7/D30 retention and monthly-active benchmarks for simulation and management games?

### Takeaway
GameAnalytics' 2026 benchmarks (16,262 mobile games, all genres) put median D7 just under 4% and median D30 ~0.7-0.8%, with top-10% games at ~40% D1, 11-12% D7 and top-1% at 13-15% D30; retention fell again in 2025. No reliable simulation-specific benchmark exists; the widely copied "simulation 45-60% D1 / 20-30% D30" figures are unsourced SEO content and conflict with the Mistplay-derived ~10% D1.

### Cited Findings
- GameAnalytics 2026 (2025 data, 16,262 mobile games with ≥1,000 MAU, iOS+Android combined, all genres aggregated): D1 top 25% just above 30%, top 10% ~40%, top 1% 64-68%; D7 median just under 4%, top 25% 6-7%, top 10% 11-12%, top 1% 25-28%; D30 median 0.69-0.79%, top 25% 1.6-1.8%, top 1% 13-15%. D1, D7 and D30 all declined in 2025 vs 2024 — [GameAnalytics via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game); PDF at [InvestGame](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-01-27-2026-mobile-pc-benchmarks_compressed.pdf)
- GameAnalytics 2025 edition (2024 data, 11,600 games, 1.48B MAU): median D1 ~22%, top 10% ~40%; median D7 ~3.4-3.9%; median D30 ~0.7%, top 10% ~4% — [GameAnalytics 2025 via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks); PDF at [InvestGame](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-GameAnalytics-Mobile-Gaming-Benchmarks.pdf)
- Engagement (mobile, 2025): daily playtime top 25% 22-24 min, top 10% 40-42 min, top 1% 94+ min; session length top 25% ~5.2 min, top 10% 8 min, top 1% ~22 min; sessions/day top 25% 5.3-5.7, top 10% ~9.6, top 1% ~14; weekend days each ~15.9% of weekly playtime vs 13-14% per weekday — [GameAnalytics via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)
- PC benchmarks (3,582 games, for comparison with a web/desktop-played manager): median daily playtime 32-33 min, median session 18 min, median sessions/day 1.65-1.7, median DAU/MAU 4-5% (top 10% 13-15%, top 1% ~40%); D1 top 25% 15-16%; D30 top 10% ~2.5% — [GameAnalytics via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game)
- Casual: D7 retention for the top-25 casual games has declined since early 2022; Tasty Travels (~22% D7) is the exception — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Unsourced genre tables list Simulation at D1 45-60%, D7 30-45%, D30 20-30% — [Juego Studio](https://www.juegostudio.com/blog/how-to-increase-user-retention-and-increase-your-games-lifetime); repeated by [Playio](https://blog.playio.co/retention-by-game-genre). Contradicted by a Mistplay-derived table citing Simulation D1 9.85%, D7 3.48% — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/mobile-gaming-app-user-retention-strategies.md). Both are far from the GameAnalytics all-genre median; treat as unreliable.
- Live-ops: event menus produced a revenue spike in 83.4% of cases, ahead of expeditions and battle passes (spike = +5% over same weekday prior week) — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)

### Inferences
- A realistic target for a daily-check-in management game: D1 30-40%, D7 10%+, D30 4%+ puts it in the top 10% of all mobile games; the all-genre median (D30 under 1%) is dominated by hypercasual churn and overstates how bad a sticky niche game will look.
- Session patterns in top-quartile games (5+ sessions/day, ~5 min each, ~23 min/day, weekend uplift) match the scheduled-event / daily-tick design well; a scheduled 8 PM race is a natural session anchor.
- PC medians (18-min sessions, 1.7 sessions/day, DAU/MAU 4-5%) are the better comparison for desktop-browser players of a web-first manager.

### Gaps
- No 2024+ genre-level retention table from GameAnalytics, Sensor Tower or AppMagic is public; the GameAnalytics 2025 PDF has a "genre performance" section that I could not read. Sim/management-specific D1/D7/D30 remains unknown.
- No MAU benchmarks for mobile at the genre level; mobile DAU/MAU medians were not stated.

## How do web-first or PWA games fare versus app-store games for this audience?

### Takeaway
Web gaming is growing fast off a small base (Poki ~100M monthly players, ~40% on mobile; Poki revenue +~50%/yr since 2020; simulator games among the fastest-growing web segments in 2025), but most browser play is still desktop, revenue is largely ad-based and far below app-store IAP, and no credible source measures PWA vs native retention or conversion.

### Cited Findings
- Poki: 625M players across desktop and mobile in 2025 — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/poki); 100M monthly players and 1B gameplays in a single month — [Mobidictum interview](https://mobidictum.com/?p=209499); earlier claim of 60M MAU and 2.6x the audience of CrazyGames — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/web-gaming-strikes-back/)
- Poki revenue has grown ~50% a year since 2020; CrazyGames' founder cited 43% average annual growth over four years and a $100M revenue target by 2027 — [Bloomberg, Nov 2025](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-07/video-games-hottest-new-platform-is-an-old-one-websites)
- Mobile share of web players: Poki says 40%, Similarweb estimates 32%; across top browser-game sites 28% of monthly uniques are mobile, ranging from <4% (EA Pogo) to 52% (agame.com); most browser gamers still play on desktop — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/web-gaming-strikes-back/)
- Simulator and Roblox-like games were the fastest-growing web segments in 2025, traffic up ~25% YoY (source of the analysis unclear) — [GameDeveloper press release](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/new-study-reveals-the-growth-engagement-discoverability-and-value-driving-web-gaming-to-outperform-gaming-giants-and-social-media)
- Poki-commissioned "2026 State of Web Gaming" study (June 2026) claims web gaming is growing while investment in game makers fell 55% in 2025; sponsored, treat with care — [InvestGame PDF](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/2026-06-25-the_2026_state_of_web_gaming_report_4ee6fdc260_wp/)
- CrazyGames launched a native mobile app in 2025 and reported hundreds of thousands of organic downloads (self-reported LinkedIn) — [LinkedIn](https://tr.linkedin.com/in/yagizefemertol)
- HTML5 games: $1.03B revenue in 2021, projected $3.09B by 2028 (Google/Kantar); WeChat minigame revenue +50% YoY in 2023; Kongregate earned 80% of revenue from IAP at its peak; web ad revenue per player is lower than mobile in-game ads (no figure); platform rev-shares 50-75% to developers — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/web-gaming-strikes-back/)
- Sensor Tower reports web-shop advertising had a positive effect in over 50% of cases (author considers this counterintuitive) — [Sensor Tower via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Newzoo reports PC browser gaming in consistent decline (no figures) — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/web-gaming-strikes-back/)
- A claim that "nearly 70% of mobile gamers access titles through browser-based or lightweight applications," attributed to Newzoo, and a claim that Miniclip PWAs out-retain native builds, both appear only on low-quality SEO pages with no primary source; not verified — [example SEO page](https://isgsommelier.vn/?p=8514)

### Inferences
- For a text/UI-heavy management game, web-first is viable for reach and discovery (Poki/CrazyGames-scale portals, zero install friction, desktop tenure), but the paying audience on mobile lives in the app stores; a PWA wrapper plus a Capacitor store build (hybrid) is the path that captures both, which matches the direction already decided for Trot Dynasty.
- Web players skew desktop and session-long (PC median 18-min sessions), which suits a program/race-night loop; mobile app players skew short frequent sessions, which suits check-in mechanics. Design for both cadences.

### Gaps
- No source measures retention, conversion, LTV or CPI for PWA vs app-store versions of the same game. No demographics for web gamers. No web-gaming revenue figure for 2025 from an independent firm.
