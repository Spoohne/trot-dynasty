Training the Youngsters
Yearlings, 2-year-olds and 3-year-olds are yours to make, and the Training tab is their day sheet. A yearling comes up in stages, one at a time: break work to get it used to the harness, then jogging. Once it's broke and jogged, its days are training miles on the focus you choose, or a turn out for a day off.
