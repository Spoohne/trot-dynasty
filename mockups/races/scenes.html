Ten scenes

Ten identical horses, directed. No stats and no result behind any of them.

  1. 1. Wire to wireClears early, rates a soft second quarter, and has plenty left. The pocket horse never gets there.
  2. 2. The pocket tripSaves every inch behind the leader, then comes off the rail in the stretch and just gets there.
  3. 3. First-over grinderPulls at the half with nobody in front of it, grinds to the leader's flank and wears it down.
  4. 4. Cover flowA line forms outside behind the first-over horse. The one on its back gets the perfect trip and sweeps by late.
  5. 5. Parked the mileLeaves hard, can't clear, and stays parked outside the leader all the way. It costs it at the end.
  6. 6. Speed duelTwo go for the lead and won't give in. Blistering fractions, both back up, and the closers come.
  7. 7. The hand-offClears to the lead, hands it over at the quarter to sit second, then comes back for it.
  8. 8. The late brushSits last, sweeps three wide round the final turn and goes by the whole field.
  9. 9. Up the passing laneRail trip all the way, then dives inside the leader where the rail swings in down the stretch.
  10. 10. Photo finishThe whole front of the field comes together in the stretch, spread across the track.
