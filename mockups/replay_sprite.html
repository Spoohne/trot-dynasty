Sprite mockup: one painted horse-and-sulky with an 8-frame pacing cycle from SpriteCook. Every runner uses the same art, with its number cloth drawn on. The legs are timed to ground speed, so they stay in step at any replay speed. The camera cuts like a broadcast director: wide start, pans, tight shots, a low stretch run, then a fixed camera at the wire.
